# What lands a PR is the project's fact

What happened: a closer could treat its own auto-merge or hold convention as the repository's landing procedure. That can bypass a merge queue, misread a hold state, or arm work the repository means to admit itself.

The rule: Start reads the landing procedure beside the project commands and captures its brake, enforcement, arm, and deciding check or queue signal. Close follows that recorded project procedure. A missing or ambiguous procedure is reported and blocks Close from guessing a landing action.

Enforced: `plugins/bottega-dex/skills/start/SKILL.md`, `plugins/bottega-dex/skills/close/SKILL.md`, and `CONTEXT.md`.
